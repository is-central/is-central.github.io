## Squad introduction

1. **Count money as double.** Shop items are half price, so entering a shop with 30 Ingots is effectively entering with 60.
2. **Do not hoard.** Ingots come easily for this squad, so small payouts (a 30-Ingot ending 2 payment, a 50-Ingot doubt) are inconsequential
3. **Watch your Coupons constantly, use them sparingly.** The squad is short of Coupons rather than Ingots. 
4. **Exchanging tongbao gives 3 ingots.** This is a large portion of ingot income especially after getting Memory Scope
5. **Guaranteed verticals in shop nodes.** Making pathing easier than other squads

Shop squad is strong at doing 245, the economy gained from f6 shop gives a huge power boost to this squad at the last shop on f6

245 has a stricter enroute restriction, requiring a scout which has no guarantee to appear or to have verticals on each floor. With the verticals from the shop, its rare to see scout not on a viable path with other important nodes. 

In 235, shop squad’s relic filtering ability is weaker and could get statchecked by ed5

Shop squad has one of the highest collectible counts compared to other squads, which gives many off-meta operators a chance to shine in a collectible-centric theme like IS6 245

## Overall Route Selection and Run Management Strategy

### Start of Run

* Prioritize starting with 2 coupons 

  * 5 coupons = 1 vertical (3 coupons) + 1 refresh (2 coupons)
* Main way to consume coupons:

  * Shop Refresh - 2 > 3 > 4
  * Retossing - 1 > 2 > 2 > 4 > 6 > 9

### Raidian Start:

E2 Raidian costs 4 hope in total, while a non-raidian start e2 costs 10 hope in total, the extra 6 hope can be used to draft an extra 6* going into bosky

Path 1 normal + 1 em for enough hope for an extra 6*, save the vertical for other floors

### Non-Raidian Start:

Do 3 fights, even 3 nm for lvl 3 command level in order to get 2 6*s for enough early wonderland power.

Shop is a **very** candle limited squad, without a strong early comp going into wonderland, its easy to waste candles and lose economy.

### Floor 1

* **F1 wish fulfilled > 1 encounter**

  * Wish fulfilled lets you choose a collectible for power, if neither are desirable, can always choose the third option to gamble either all, rare collectible or fail for 8 ingots (16 ingots)
  * The f1 encounter pool has 6 possibilities, with “Duck Guardian” (bear fight) and “Seclusion” (2 coupon 2 Max LP with Raidian) being more rewarding than wish fulfilled
  * Choose 2 coupons for “Fork in the Road”, coupons > ingots in all cases
  * The worst encounters to see on f1 are the “All are Welcome” and “Communication” encounters. 
* **Combats**

  * DLC2 gives all combats to give a chance to drop an additional 4 ingot tongbao, equivalent to +3 ingots (6 ingots)
  * Pay attention to statuegeist and chest drops in stages for extra ingot gain
* **Floor 1: Strategy 1:**

  * Conserve f1 vouchers, use them on f2 Pathfinder and draft directly via vouchers otherwise
  * Suitable for non-Raidian starters
  * Draft one operator if f1 path has an encounter on it to get em bear fight
  * Only scenario is if first encounter is on the second column and the first voucher lets you e2 your starter, then conserve
* **Floor 1: Strategy 2:**

  * Draft directly throughout F1 and do the pathfinder fight on F2

### Floor 2 Wonderland

**Plan your pathing by calculating how many candle steps are required to fully explore the wonderland.**

Default candles by the end of f3 is 15 (16 with Raidian), excluding additional sources:

* Floor 1 Wonderland

  * 3 Candles
* Floor 2 Wonderland

  * 5 Candles
* Floor 3 Wonderland

  * 3 Candles
* Great Tomb Whistle (Shop Enter)

  * 3 Candles
* Skill Tree

  * 1 Candle
* Raidian

  * 1 Candle

Extra candles can be used for turmoil failures, re-entering nodes like plan but your ultimate goal is to fully explore in 14 candles and 1 more to enter tavern to memory scope.

This is important for wage war/define a source since the tavern needs to be found.

Collectibles that reveal nodes (wu shang shuttle, yin shih flask, shen chu wheel, tzu wu blade) are strong since they reduce the number of steps required to fully explore the wonderland.

Always pick yin shih flask over the other two relics in “Times change” encounter.

#### **Losing candles via turmoils is unacceptable**

Control the ed3 doubt node by choosing the path with the least number of possible blocked nodes first. 

For 235 players its recommended to get 5 candleguests before f3 to ensure ed3 doesn’t whiff.

### Nodes:

**Trade node:** Buy Unsealed Case if needed, Awakening Tower if needed, other collectibles if needed.

**Legend node:** Take 5 steps elsewhere before legend nodes to fully exploit the Campfire encounter, except if yin shih flask is owned

**Turmoils:** 

* Evaluate your squad and collectibles to determine your odds, avoid turmoils that waste candles or do not reveal other nodes.
* Turmoils are the main source of coupons, but tackling turmoils early is not optimal due to:

  * Lose stacks on stir fry and tzu wu blade
  * Save turmoils for a late ed2 foot killing



**Doubts:** Prioritize 4yan/2yan to proc Hai shih hour to block a debuff hour on f7

**Plan:** 

Exchanging tongbaos via LP/exchange all does not give 3 ingots via the squad passive & you must have at least 13 ingots to reflair 3 times, costs 10 ingots

**Reflairing** is usually a late-run decision to refresh one-time use tongbaos like, degradable tongbaos or strong economy/combat tongbaos.

* Any reflair (gain an additional use)

  * Degradable tongbaos (Dawn of Yan, Step by Step)
  * One-time use tongbaos (Way In, Garden Trip, Tactile Token)
* Flair with Attract for increased toss chance

  * Economy tongbaos (steel and soil, people united, body honed, waterworks, cocoon worth)
  * Combat tongbaos (bloodshed, zuo le, law and ritual)
* Flair with Light or Attract, flair is kept upon upgrade, light procs an additional time

  * Upgradable tongbaos (people united, body honed)
* Any reflair (gain another initial one-time use)

  * One time resource tongbaos (Gift of accord, debts unending, odd merchant)

**Exchange all:**

* Exchange all flower after memory scope, exchange all risk to look for combat power


* Expends extra candles, make sure wonderland is fully explored and endings are guaranteed



**Memory scope (Tavern):**

Prioritize hope is fully expended over ingots fully expended & ingots can be paid into ed2 and ingot income is not a huge issue.

## Floor 3

#### General enroute:

1. Ceaseless (Pathfinder) f3 for coupons to relieve coupon pressure


2. Spend ingots in Shop, spend all hope
3. Take verticals for strong nodes like Faceoff, Wonderland, Shop
4. Prioritize encounters over normal combats from f3 onwards
5. Faceoff is the lowest tier option but is the best option for the tongbao drop & help stack bells

Relic sifting in shops is merchant squad’s strongest tool to find run-defining relics such as the big 4 (Stir fry, Gu qin, Bells, Drum).

In comparison to the f1 shop, the more ingots one has (80+) the more preferable to buy out entire pages of the shop to narrow the collectible pool for strong relics.

When relic sifting, prioritize buying boss pool relics and 8 ingot relics, however, do keep in mind what type of stats your current run lacks (dp, initial sp, sp regen etc).



#### F3 no shop scenario:

1. Reserve coupons for f4 for refreshing/retossing tactile token, memory scope on f4 shop


2. All tongbaos should be picked up for the +3 ingot squad passive
3. The coffer must have a single coin slot for constantly swapping new coins
4. Faceoff is the lowest tier option but is the best option for the tongbao drop & help stack bells



## Floor 4:

Pathfinder on f4 can be skipped if there is no candleguest pressure in wonderland, or doing the pathfinder fight but putting an already candleguested operator on the pad just to get the combat rewards.

With Sui's flower or Bells, everybody can be candleguested.

This is also where 235 players do clarity in case f5 wonderland and pathfinder are not on the same path (or pathfinder not spawning on f5).

F4 wonderland is the place to do recorded memory fight (ed5 prereq).



## Floor 5:

Your first stat check is ED2/ED2a.

* If ED2 has not been acquired, save 30 ingots before entering wonderland, F5 shop and wonderland are both guaranteed spawns
* Ranking nodes in the following order in terms of ingot gain:

  * Faceoff (2 tongbaos) > Pathfinder > EM > Encounter > Nm > Wish fulfilled 
  * Safehouse for 2 coupons
  * Lost and found to exchange awakening tower or whistle for 16 cost relics
  * Exchange all is not recommended
  * Include shop and wonderland on your pathing
* Awakening hours

  * Spend ingots beforehand -> Pay ingots to awaken hour in tavern
