---
title: IS6 Draft Guide
description: Guide for drafting operators in IS6
---
Below is an analysis of 6* operators in the context of consistent D18 IS6 play, going below D18 doesn't change how good operators are relatively, it just makes the run (much) less difficult

This guide was written with Persona collab as the latest event released in CN in September 2026.

Enroute: Enroute is a term for the combats up until ending bosses, but this guide will use to refer only to combats in the garden and not turmoil fights for clarity.

Turmoil: Turmoil is the term for fights in Sui Remnant Consciousness/Bosky, being able to do them in 1-2 fights early on is essential to not get pathlocked which is vital for winning consistently.

Starter: Starters in this guide are operators that at E2 can duo with E2 Raidian or trio with E1 Raidian + another E1 starter to handle enough turmoils to make pathing consistent, since one should always start Raidian unless one wants to borrow an op they don't have or playing a squad that allows an E2 starter. They also happen to be good enroute.

Pathfinder: Pathfinder combats are very good since they give strong rewards and should always be considered, with using conserved vouchers mainly in Bosky entries a very viable strategy.

Hitcount: IS6 is heavy on hitcount enemies, but everything enroute can be solved by Raidian S2, so this guide will discuss ED2 and Beyond Mount and Sea, which are the stages where Raidian isn't sufficient for hitcount.

Bodies: Body in this guide means the tails in the enhanced versions of ED5 (Stasis), which reflect damage onto one's units requiring dedicated ops to solve quickly, if taking Merciless 2+ body solves is ideal since you'll likely be overwhelmed by enemies, otherwise, 1 is sufficient.

Shuo also wants to be solved by many of the same ops since they dodge attacks by units not blocking them and does extreme damage in ED5 on higher difficulties.

Exusiai The New Covenant: Exusiai is highly recommended for advanced IS6 due to incredible enroute, turmoil, and ending performance. S2's barrier can allow any operator to survive pathfinders, and it’s cycle makes it an incredible skill even at E1. S3 has similar damage and combos incredibly well with Hoshiguma to unleash S3 twice and is an incredible body solution. S2 can also very consistently tank ED2 slaps below D18. (Starter)

Tragodia: Tragodia is a unique operator that isn't that strong for turmoils and can be redundant for enroute sometimes, but is incredibly important for ED5 and is a viable ED4 Ju solve. S1 is only used for ED4 as a Ju solve, SL7 and M3 needs \~90 ASPD and \~45 ASPD to permastall respectively without -SP effects. S2 is good as always for map wide presence and CC, with an incredible synergy with -redeploy items. S3 is what makes Tragodia so good for ED4/5 as a Ju and Shuo stall, and being able to handle ED5 flames/swords somewhat well. S3 also scales incredibly well with SP buffs, with -40% making it extremely strong, and it can permastall, with the exact breakpoint being unique to each situation.


Hoshiguma The Breacher: Hoshiguma is by far the best defender in IS6, due to S3 providing incredible enroute, turmoil, and ending power. S3 also provides incredible utility in pathfinder fights, making them much easier to survive, and can be cycled to completely negate ED2 with -redeployment bonuses. S2 is rather situational but is still somewhat useful but cannot handle hitcount. (Starter)

Mon3tr: Unfortunately Mon3tr is solely used as a body solve and a potential Shuo DPS, otherwise they generally aren't worth the hope, though they are nice to handle ED3 HP drain while doing something useful.

Pramanix The Prerita: Pramanix is the best caster in IS6 because they can handle turmoils and has strong enroute with S2/3 doing mostly the same thing with different uptimes, so pick whichever is better for the current stage. They also have the ability to handle hitcount with S1 to push enemies away, but may need help with actually defeating them. (Starter)

Entelachia: Entelechia is a powerful starter, laneholder, and hitcount operator with S2, especially with their Y module. However they fall off at D18 and stop being able to function as a starter due to the increased stats, and ED5 also disfavors them.

Kirin R Yato: Yato is incredibly strong for ED5 being a great option for bodies and scaling very well with law and ritual, and since their turmoil performance is somewhat weak it is recommended to not candleguest Yato if you pick up ceaseless to ensure you can solve bodies. Yato also is viable as an ED5 Arrogance DPS with good damage.

Angelina: Though Angelina is not as strong in IS6 as IS2/3/4, they are still a very solid option because of their excellent cycle and good damage, with very good crowd control and hitcount as well. However they have heavy competition from Tragodia, Raidian, and Gnosis for supporter vouchers, need E2 to function and are outclassed by gnosis in their main niche.

Titi: Titi is a notable draft because of S2, which is a viable hitcount solution and cycles very well with ED2 slaps letting you keep another operator safe. S2’s main use is as a strong combo with Wang letting Wang act as a Body solve in ED5, which is the main reason to draft Titi.

Silverash The Reignfrost: Silveralt is the best vanguard and an important draft due to having very strong turmoil performance and great enroute performance and crowd control in ED5. S3 is Silveralt’s main skill in turmoils due to it’s alright damage and very strong fragile, with the DP swap being almost irrelevant. S2 is the main skill for enroute and endings for CC with freeze and DP reduction. Both are also good for dealing ED4 invisible enemies that spawn on D16+. (Starter)

Hoolheyak: Hool is a DPS option for ED4's Hou and ED5's Arrogance Embodied, where they are a viable solution with alpha module. Otherwise their crowd control isn't the best and probably shouldn't be drafted.

Raidian: Raidian is the strongest starter by far due to incredible enroute and turmoil and ED1 performance, with a strong ED2/3 performance as well, though they are notably weaker in ED4/5 boss fights they are still very useful for consistently doing well in IS6. S2 is Raidian’s main skill due to having a very balanced combination of damage, blocking, and placement, as well as handling all hitcount checks enroute. S1 is Raidian's secondary skill for it’s incredible stalling potential which is useful enroute, for ED5 prerequisite and ED5 itself, and for surviving pathfinders. S3 is their tertiary skill and should only be used when the main threats of the stage are flying. An important thing to know is that Raidian’s α module is bugged and its -redeploy effect stacks between redeployments, letting them spam summons with very little cooldown, though if one places both a summon and Raidian during pause deploy it won't get the current stack. (Starter)

Surtr: Surtr should be exclusively drafted as a potential Body and Shuo solution and isn't really meta for enroute or other endings.

Kal'tsit Esperanta: Esperanta is the best 6* medic in IS6 because they let you use medic vouchers for a starter and being reliable in general. S2 is Esperanta’s main skill that does solid damage with great slow, and is very strong for opening ED3 and several turmoils, and providing incredible healing for pathfinders. S3’s main use is letting Esperanta cheese pathfinders by moving themselves off the stand but still being lit, with it also being decent for redeploying Body solves or other ops in ED5. Takeoff is also good for ignoring ED2 slaps. (Starter)

Gnosis: Gnosis is very strong as a hitcount and CC operator with their α module. S2 is the main skill with an incredible cycle that gives an incredible freeze uptime two-thirds without SP buffs. S3 is mainly notable as a way to stall Ju in ED4 to let you DPS them effectively. Gnosis’s fragile is also very impactful, and the α module also makes Silveralt S2 vastly better at stalling and adds fragile and hitcount too.

Wang: Wang is an incredible operator for turmoils, enroute, and doing damage to bosses. S2 is just as useable as S3 with strong CC and having a great cycle, while still doing good damage. S3 is incredible damage with great range, but has no utility and an awkward cycle. Wang is able to handle almost every turmoil at E2 with an additional advantage of being able to handle ED5 flames/swords, and even Bodies with help from Titi or Hoshi.

Haruka: Haruka is somewhat of a trap operator since they likely won’t hit any major breakpoints to allow your units to survive things they wouldn't otherwise, and the supporter voucher being very competitive in IS6, with Purestream and Esperanta also being viable for ED3 healing.

Sakiko: Saki is a strong DPS with S3 but is somewhat lacking in other utility and turmoil performance. S3 has strong damage but aside from being a Body solution doesn’t solve much in endings unlike many other DPS options like Wang, Exusiai, and Hoshiguma. S3’s mixed damage is also a downside that can cancel out the RES/DEF ignore.

Ch’en The Dawnstreak: Chen is a very useful operator as the best DPS for ED2, Hou in ED4, and Arrogance in ED5. S3 is Chen’s only good skill and does great damage in general and is incredible for large bosses, with ED2/2a/4/5 all having good tiles for S3. 

Leizi The Thunderbringer. Leizi is notable for being a starter that can function well at E1, unlike most other starters, though despite being good enroute they aren't that good against endings. S2 is Leizi’s main skill since IS6 generally has open spaces for it to scale. S3 is the secondary skill for stages with tight corridors and functions as a hitcount solve, with a decent advantage of cycling to negate ED2 slaps. (Starter)

Ulpianus: Ulpianus is bad in IS6, since Pseudomuts appear in many stages and hard counter ground ops with low range, and also has a weak turmoil performance and doesn't work well for ED5.

Lappland The Decandenza: Lappland is solely a DPS op and lacks real utility, since global range doesn't function as a good solution to any endings in IS6, they also have an awkward cycle, Raidian also has very good global presence which is sufficient for most stages.

Wiš'adel: Wis is notable as the best sniper currently and being a viable starter at E2, while also being viable hitcount solve with S2. S3 is very solid damage with strong survival, but it’s awkward cycle holds Wis back from being fully optimal, along with completely needing E2 unlike other starters. (Starter)

Archetto: Archetto suffers from their best two partners, Mon3tr and Saki, not being that good in IS6 and the rest of them having much better skills or not being good enough to make Archetto viable.

Shu: Shu suffers in IS6 from the theme lacking anything that needs healing above Sussurro and scaling from items and tongbaos makes S3’s buffs negligible, though they are alright if you don't have better ops for ED3.
