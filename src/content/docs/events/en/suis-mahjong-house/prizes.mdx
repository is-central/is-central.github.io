Yostar will be sponsoring the prizes

* 1st place team:
    * Each player gets the latest artbook: https://us.yostar.store/collections/arknights/products/arknights-arkninghst-official-artworks-vol-3
    * Each player gets a \$20 google play gift card
* 2nd-4th place teams: 
    * Each player gets a Yunomi Teacup: https://us.yostar.store/collections/arknights/products/arknights-yunomi-teacup-6th-anniv
    * Each player gets a \$10 google play gift card
* Non finalist teams will also have the chance to be signed up to a raffle from which 4 teams will be selected, each member receiving a \$5 monthly card
    * Each team gains 1 raffle entry upon registering and playing on the preliminary round
    * Each completed run will grant the team 1 entry to the raffle
    * If a team is selected for the raffle, all 3 players of the team will receive the prize of a monthly card
    * Up to 4 teams or 12 players can win the raffle
    * If a team qualifies for the finals they are no longer eligible for the raffle
    * Each team can only win the raffle once
    * Raffle drawing will be done during the finals of the tournament
    * To receive the monthly card prize, a Tournament Organizer can:
        * PayPal the equivalent amount of money
        * Send an Amazon Giftcard
        * Log on the players account to perform the purchase directly

## Final scoring
* This scoring system will be used twice throughout the tournament:
    * First time will be to qualify for the finals.
    * Second time will be to decide the finals placements for the top 4 prizes.
* After the 3rd player finishes their run, the team can now spend all the accumulated Dollars (\$) on a Summon Simulator.
* A player from the team must stream the following website <a href="https://is-central.github.io/tools/gacha-sim/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">IS Central gacha simulator</a>
* The team will insert the amount of Dollars accumulated and can select any banner from the simulator.
    * If the amount of Dollars introduced is higher than it should be, or reset history is clicked, the team risks being disqualified.
* The summon simulator follows the same rules and rates as the in-game summoning system such as pity.
* Teams will receive score based on the rarity of the operators recruited:
    * For Every 6 star operator summoned = 360 pts
    * For Every 5 star operator summoned = 120 pts
    * For Every 3 and 4 star operator summoned = 10 pts
