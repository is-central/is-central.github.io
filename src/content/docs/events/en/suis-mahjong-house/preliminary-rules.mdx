## Introduction
Sui's Mahjong House is a team-based tournament with teams of 3 players that consists of earning money to win.
Players earn dollars and yan by completing objectives during their runs, yan can be spent to unlock buffs for their runs while dollars can be converted into placement points using a summon simulator at the end.
The top 5 teams proceed to Round 2; the final placement is also leaderboard-based.


## Format
* Teams of 3 players
  * Player 1 and 2: Guided Tour 15
  * Player 3: Guided Tour 18
* Tournament is divided in:
  * Preliminary phase
  * Finals
* Leaderboard:
  * Top 5 advance to the Finals
  * Final placement is determined by the Finals leaderboard


## Placement points
Teams will earn Money in Dollars by completing tasks and challenges.
The Money earned is accumulated across the team and will be used in a summon simulator after player 3 finishes their run.


## Rules
### Run Preparation
* Players may prepare their run prior to their scheduled match.
* Only the following Floor 3 bonus buffs are allowed: Kettle, 2 Hope, 2 Coupons.
* Players may see the 1st floor layout and the tongbaos tossed.

### Balance Limit Withdrawal
There is no ingot balance withdrawal limit for the first 2 shops entered. No withdrawals allowed after.

### Reset
All players start with 0 available resets.
Emulator crashes: each player allowed 1 restart per tournament (subject to organizer review).

### Time limit
Players are allotted 3 hours and 30 minutes for their entire run. If a player enters Ending 5 (any variant) the timer pauses.

Penalty is \%500 for every 15 minutes of overtime before run ends or ED5 is reached.

### Miscellaneous
* Players are allowed to openly communicate at all times during their runs.
* Players may only use the account they used to register with.
* All bonuses and penalties will be calculated at the end of the run.
* A player can have at most 5 kettles. -15$ for every kettle that exceeds 5.

### Team Challenges
#### Winds
As a team, enter the floor 3 boss while having the following coins active:
* Risk-Northern Sting
* Risk-Southern Mounts
* Risk-Eastern Gap
* Risk-West Stones

Reward: \$300 if 3 tongbaos, \$500 if all 4 tongbaos

#### Flowers
As a team complete the 4 following challenges:
* Plume: use Plume's trait to generate 4 dp per stage on 3 dreadful foe stages
* Orchid: kill 4 enemies in a single deployment
* Podenco: kill 4 apparitions in a single deployment
* Matoimaru: kill an elite enemies in 4 stages 

Reward: for each challenge, increase the Yan gained by a multiplier of $\times$1.1, up to $\times$1.5 if all Flower challenges are completed.
That is 1.1, 1.21, 1.331, 1.5 for 1, 2, 3, and 4 challenges completed respectively.
This multiplier will apply retroactively.

#### Seasons
* Winter Equip 4 Icefield Messenger skins in a single run and deploy all 4 operators in a single stage (once per team)
* Summer Equip 4 Coral Coast skins in a single run and deploy all 4 operators in a single stage (once per team)
* Fall Equip 4 Witch Feast skins in a single run and deploy all 4 operators in a single stage (once per team)
* Spring Equip 4 Vitafield skins in a single run and deploy all 4 operators in a single stage (once per team)

Reward: \$200 per challenge
