## How to qualify
* Players must register as a team.
* Players 1 and 2 must submit a screenshot of a run that completed the ending "No regrets" at Guiding Tour 15
* Player 3 must submit a screenshot of a run that completed the ending "No regrets" at Guiding Tour 18
* All runs must be completed after Mon, 16 Nov 2026 19:00:00 GMT

![Screenshot example](/events/suis-mahjong-house/qualify-example.png)

* A Player of the team must be a member of the IS Central Discord
* Players must be able to stream their runs on Discord/Youtube/Twitch
* Registration Form: https://tinyurl.com/suimahjonghouse
