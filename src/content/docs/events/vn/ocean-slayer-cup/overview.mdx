Clear **Ending 4 (Glory of Humanity)** in **IS3** at **difficulty 18**. Everyone who slays the ocean receives a **Monthly Card**. The 3 highest scores also receive a **Monthly Headhunting Pack**.

Register for a time slot, record your run, and upload it. You only get one chance. If you can't record, message the organizers.

## Rules

- You may play on an EN, JP, TC or KR account. SC accounts are not allowed.
- If you disconnect or quit the run for any reason, it counts as a loss. The only exception is your one reset (below).

### Opener

Pick one:

- **Floor 3 Bonus start:** take Hot Water Kettle or 2 Hope. You must be on the Floor 3 bonus selection screen when you start your run.
- **Class Squad start:** start with the promoted operator of your choice on a Class Squad. You cannot have a Floor 3 bonus.

### Reset

You may reset **once**, and only when you can enter the **Floor 3 Boss Node** (before fighting it). Your run starts over from an opener of your choice, and only the run you finish counts.

To reset, **do not stop recording**. Exit the run, go to **Archives > Training Grounds**, then quit the run and prepare your opener.

## Recording

- Start on the Arknights home screen to show the server time. It must be within 180 seconds of your scheduled time (organizers have final say).
- Open the IS3 tab and begin your run from your prepared opener.
- When you finish, show your operators, collectibles and the end screen. Click the end screen **once** to display your score.
- After the score finishes displaying, return to the home screen to verify the run time.
- Record continuously, from start to end, at a watchable quality and frame rate. If the video is too poor, we may be unable to score your run.

## Scoring

Scoring only decides the 3 Monthly Headhunting Packs. Clearing the challenge earns the Monthly Card regardless of score.
