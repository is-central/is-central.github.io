The stage consists of enemies from Invitation to Wine aka every casual’s favourite enemy type: hitcount.

Here’s a breakdown of enemies with the count and type of hitcount enemies they spawn after dying:

* Grindstone: Spawns 2 Wooden Seal on death, each requiring 3 hits from any damage source
* Brightmirror: Spawns 2 Rosewood Seal on death, each requiring 5 hits from any damage source
* Fountainboil: Spawns 4 Celadon Teaware on death, each requiring 6 hits from only arts and true sources. You have to deal with only 1-2 of these
* Inkwave: Spawns 15 Wooden Paperweight on death, each requiring 5 hits from any source
* Heartbillow: Spawns 15 Rosewood Paperweight on death, each requiring 6 hits from any source

All enemies make a beeline for objective point unless their paths are manipulated with the 3 roadblocks. Topmost lane has a Talisman traveling every 20-25 seconds

**Notable differences between NM and EM**

A statuegeist and few more Inkwave and Heartbillow than NM. Statuegeists can mess up roadblock positions depending on where they spawn, same for ingot chests but they are rather trivial

## Notable Enemies

* If you are Raidian-less AND have no hitcount solution, glhf with every enemy on this stage
* If you do not have anything for Fountainboils, do NOT kill them, otherwise what could have been only 1-2 leaks would become 4-8 leaks

## Notable Debuffs

Taking on Sui foot in this stage can be tricky considering the normal solution is to stack summons together in this stage. Incidentally, while roadblocks cannot direct the foot’s attention themselves, they do add to the body count when an operator is placed beside them.

## Notable Operators

* Hitcount solutions ranging from Ethan to Pramanix the Prerita
* If you have Raidian you need only something for Celadon Teaware, multi-target arts/true damage units with crowd control/some aspd can deal with them eg Wang, Kaltsit the Esperanta
* Alternative to second point, Radian with Start+ module can deal with them, in fact she solos the stage relicless upto NM D15

## Stage Strategy with Raidian

strategy 1 image

* Use s2. Activate skil only for Inkwaves and Heartbillows
* Place roadblocks and summons as shown above
* Your Fountainboil dealer’s position can differ depending on who it is, for eg Ascalon/Ethan would like to be at C8 if no healing/D7 if you have heal support, Pramanix the Prerita can be placed at D10/C10, Hoshiguma the Breacher at C4/D4 (can work at middle as well)
* Killing enemies near roadblocks is not recommended since furnitures can path through them
* Placing roadblocks too late aka when enemy has almost reached it results in them destroying the roadblock
* Mimic chests can be stopped from moving by surrounding them with roadblocks
