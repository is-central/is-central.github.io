# Abstract


This small guide made for people


* who want to simply get all theme rewards and forget about it
* who want to mess with gamemode (leak all stages, destroy bosses in 2 seconds)
* have problems with some Raidian non-squad related missions
* just simpy want to fast unlock all nodes in Ageless Erudition (skill tree of this theme)


This strategy has 2 versions: 


* Easy version - works only on **Difficutly 11 and lower** due to coins retoss cost increase on Difficulty 12, less random and actually follows normal squad gameplay strategy
* Harder version - works untill **Difficulty 17**, relates on Interdependent Luck relic


DLC2 (aka Ed5/Chongue/Wage war) update and implementation of Difficulty 18 made this squad alot weaker due to even bigger coins retoss costs (1 coupon on first retoss, 2 on second, 4 on third, 6 on fourth, 9 on fifth and onward), but its still strong squad for casual and low difficulty runs. Also, addition of trade-all tongbao option made this strat even easier.


# Basic things what you should already know


Main mechanic of this theme, what should be considered during plaing this theme, is how plan node exchange work, and taking advantage from it:


1. All coins are separated into 3 categories/rarities - normal, rare and super rare
2. During trading in plan node, you can exchange coin of same rarity into different one (with some encounter-related exceptions)
3. Certain trades can result into coin rarity increase, such as:


* Trading any rare coin can result into getting Flower-The Bulwark coin (super rare)


4. Certain trades can result into coin rarity decrease. such as:


* Trading any super rare coin can result into:
* a) Getiing one of three rare coins - Risk‐Hidden Gambit, Bal‐Tactile Token and Flower‐The Way In
* b) Getting one of two normal coins - Flower‐Martial Fight and Flower‐Trades
* Trading any rare coin can result into getting one of four normal coins - Flower‐Long Road, Risk‐Study, Bal‐Testee and Bal‐Prevention
* You cant have 2 same coins in a box with exception for 6 general coins - Bal‐Soil to Metal, 	Bal‐Water to Wood, Bal‐Metal to Water, Flower‐Fire to Soil, Bal‐Wood to Fire and Dayan Tongbao (any tongbao what have **Turns into Dayan Tongbao after** description turns into it obviously)

# Getting started


Effects of our squad are next:


1. Every trade in plan node costs 1 life point (doesnt icreases after trades)
2. If you toss at least 1 flower coin, you will get 1 coupon (works during entering Bosky, retossing coins)
3. You always start with Bal‐A People United coin (when tossed, can refresh any node with exception of boss node and Prophecy node into random one), what could be upgraded into Flower‐Ideas Carried (2 refreshes instead of 1)


Flower squad gameplay relies heavily on using plan node coins exchange in Sui`s Remnant Consciousness (aka special Bosky node of this theme), constantly exchanging existing coins into stronger ones (in both combat stage and economic power) and abusing rules what was listed before.


# How Loop works


Entire strategy revolves around synergy between 2 things:


* +1 coupon if flower coin was tossed
* First effect of Bal‐A Body Honed (and upgraded version Flower‐Mindfulness) - **getting Max Life Points on toss**


The trick is - coins retoss will cost 0 coupons till we tossing at least 1 flower coin. So we can just spam retoss button, swim in Life Points, and spend them in either stages by leaking all enemies and bosses or buyout Ji`s trade shop, increasing our power level in run.


# Easy version of Loop


1. During play, find Bal‐A Body Honed and later trade it for Flower‐Mindfulness in Plan node (you can find it in Face-off node with chosing easy version of fight, which will give 2 choices between 2 coins, can find it randomly after combat, in Trade node in Bosky, in normal Cannot ignots shop.
2. Trade your Bal‐A People United for Flower‐Ideas Carried with Plane node trade (to increase chances of tossing at least 1 flower coin during retoss).
3. While looking for Bal‐A Body Honed, try to secure next 6 coins - Flower‐Martial Fight, Flower‐Trades, Flower‐Long Road, Risk‐Study, Bal‐Testee and Bal‐Prevention (mentioned before). This step made to block any downgrade trade chance from rare to normal coin.
4. Fill remaining slots with flower coins to have better chances of tossing at least one or with prefered combat coins (like Risk‐Bloodshed).
5. Start retossing your coins to get sweet Life Points.
6. With enought LP. use them in Plan node to get better flower coins (**do not trade 6 mentioned before coins to prevent downgrade!**), while aiming to get Flower‐The Bulwark coin, which can be traded for Flower‐Cocoon Worth coin (On toss, +1 Ingot for every 4 Ingots owned).
7. During process of trading, look on candles count to not be suddenly tossed from Bosky (they could be randomly refilled with some coin effects like Light condition or Bal‐Dawn of YAN toss, just dont this coin after that and just trade in Plan node)
8. Continue retossing coin, buy anything from Trade Ji`s shop node, leak anything you want.
Some steps can change their placement depending on run coin drops (like getting Flower‐The Bulwark early, then you should have 6 coins from step 3 before trying to trade it for other super rare coins).

# Harder version of Loop


Due to Difficulty 12, which increases second coin retoss coupon cost, we can`t simply retoss our box, because at some point we will run out of coupons. Which is why we must introduce one of (if not most) broken economic relics in this mode: **Interdependent Luck**.


This relic effect is simple - you get 1 coupon each time you add risk (red) coin in your coin box.


With this simple effect, during Plane node trade, we can increase our coupons count, and use them to retoss coins even more or use them for vertical travel on normal floors.


Harder version of Loop is a bit tricky due to at some points, you could run out coupons or candles, but 80% of time its fine untill you looking on their count.


# Coins what good to save for usage instead of trading


Bal‐Dawn of YAN - On toss, +4 Coupons when in Garden of Grotesqueries, or +2 Candles when in Sui's Remnant Consciousness (**Turns into a Dayan Tongbao after**). Main purpose - candles replenish for more Plane/Trade node enter


Flower‐The Way In - On toss in Garden of Grotesqueries, can refresh nodes to Wander into Wonderland with entry to Shifei Border, once only. Main purpose - more Bosky visits (**should be traded after usage**).


Bal‐Tactile Token - When tossed in Garden of Grotesqueries, can refresh nodes to Rogue Trader, once only. Main usage - spending ignots for more relics and replacing other coins if needed 
(**should be traded after usage**).


Flower‐Garden Trip - When in Coffer, Operators return from Scout nodes with more Coupons; On toss, in Garden of Grotesqueries, can refresh nodes to Scout nodes, once only. (Apply for a lamp-holder option awards +4 coupons, Apply for a candle-wielder awards +6 coupons). Main purpose - coupons replenish/free operator promote.


Flower‐Tianshi Lin - On toss, +2 Life Points, and gain 1 Temporary Life Point each battle. Main purpose - replenishing used Life Points after trades = more trades = less candles used.


Flower‐Cocoon Worth - On toss, +1 Ingot for every 4 Ingots owned (capped at 99 per toss). Main source of ignots during loop.
